Master Data Briefing

Setting up Whirlpool
in the new ERP

REF  MASTER‑SETUP‑01
For owner review

One customer, many sites. This page walks through how Whirlpool's Pune, Kolkata and Guwahati locations should be entered — and why that's a better structure than the old system's separate record per city.

01

The old way vs. the new way

In the previous ERP, every Whirlpool site was its own, unrelated customer record — 20 rows, 20 credit limits, no shared history. The new ERP separates who we bill from where we pick up, so all 20 sites roll up under one company.

Old ERP — flat list
Whirlpool of India Ltd BhiwandiBhiwandi
Whirlpool Of India LimitedSalem
Whirlpool of India Ltd Pune IndospaceRanjangaon
Whirlpool of India Ltd‑KolkataKolkata
… 16 more, each separate—
Every site has its own credit limit and balance — the owner can't see Whirlpool's total exposure in one place.
New ERP — one company, many sites
Whirlpool of India LimitedCustomer
  ↳ PuneLocation
  ↳ KolkataLocation
  ↳ GuwahatiLocation
  ↳ … 17 more sites, same company—
One credit limit, one TDS rate, one running balance for all of Whirlpool — each site still keeps its own address and contact.

04

How it's used when booking an order

Example: Whirlpool wants a truck from their Pune plant to their Kolkata warehouse. Both ends are the same customer — just two different locations.

Pickup
Whirlpool — Pune
In transit
LR issued

Whirlpool — Kolkata
Order
Customer = Whirlpool
Pickup Location = Pune
Branch
From = your Pune branch
To = your Kolkata branch
Consignment
Loading = Pune
Unloading = Kolkata
Before entering real data
  1. Confirm this structure with the owner in the presentation — 1 Whirlpool customer, 20 locations underneath.
  2. After sign-off: create/verify States and Cities for all 20 sites.
  3. Create the single Whirlpool customer record (PAN, GST, credit limit, TDS rate).
  4. Add all 20 sites as Customer Locations under it, matching the old ERP's addresses.
  5. Test one Order end-to-end: Pune → Kolkata, before rolling out the rest.
