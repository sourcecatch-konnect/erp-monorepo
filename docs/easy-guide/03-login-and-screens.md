# 3. Login, menu and screens

> Last checked: 2026-10-12 · For: everyone

## Signing in

1. Open the SKERP website in your browser (Chrome or Edge).
2. Type your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Click **Sign in**.

**Forgot your password?** Click **Forgot password?**, type your email, and open the link that arrives within **30 minutes**. The link works only once. If no email comes, ask your admin to reset your password.

**Your login is personal.** Everything you do is recorded with your name. Never share your password.

## The menu (left side)

The menu is grouped by type of work:

| Menu group | What is inside |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Dashboard (starting page) |
| **Operations** | Orders, Lorry Receipts, Deliveries, LR Unloading Report, VP Management (rail) |
| **Container** | Trips, Vehicle Journeys, Wagon Tracking |
| **Master Data** | Masters: customers, trucks, drivers, routes, rates… |
| **Finance** | Billing & Receipts, Vendor Payments, Driver Payments, Cash & Bank, Books of Account |
| **Reports** | Vehicle P&L, Vehicle Performance, Vehicle Costs |
| **Workshop** | Purchase Orders, Stock, Job Cards, Service Bills |
| **Administration** | Settings: users, roles, notifications, audit log |

You see **only the menus your role allows**. If a colleague sees a menu you don't, that is normal (see Chapter 4).

## Three kinds of screens

Almost every module in SKERP has the same three screens. Learn them once.

### 1. List screen

Shows all records, e.g. all orders.

- **Search box:** type a number, name or city to find a record.
- **Filters:** narrow the list (by status, date, branch…).
- **Status badge:** the coloured label on each row (e.g. *Pending approval*, *Confirmed*).
- **Create / New** button: opens the form to make a new record.
- **Click a row** to open its detail screen.

### 2. Form screen

Where you **create** or **edit** a record.

- Fields with a red **\*** must be filled.
- Drop-downs take their choices from the **masters**. If what you need is not in the list, it has not been added to the masters yet. Ask your admin or manager.
- Click **Save**. If something is wrong, the ERP shows a message **next to the field** or at the top. Read it: it tells you exactly what is missing.
- If you leave a form without saving, the ERP asks *"Discard unsaved changes?"*.

### 3. Detail screen

Shows **one record** in full, e.g. one order.

- The **status** is at the top.
- **Action buttons** (Approve, Create LR, Finalise, Cancel…) appear only when that action is allowed **for this status** and **for your role**.
- **Timeline:** who did what, and when.

**If a button you expect is missing**, it is because of one of two things:
1. the record is in the wrong **status** for that action (e.g. you can't make an LR before the order is approved), or
2. your **role** doesn't allow it.

## Notifications

The **bell** at the top shows your alerts (e.g. an order waiting for your approval). Click one to open the record.

## Good habits

- **Check before you save.** Some documents can't be changed after they are finalised.
- **Never make a second copy** to fix a mistake. Tell your manager. The ERP has proper ways to correct things.
- **Use the search box** before creating a new customer, truck or driver. It may already exist.
