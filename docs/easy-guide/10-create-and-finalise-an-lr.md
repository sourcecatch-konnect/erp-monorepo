# 10. Create and finalise an LR

> Last checked: 2026-10-12 · For: booking / operations staff

This chapter takes one truck from a confirmed order to a **finalised LR** that the driver can carry.

## The steps at a glance

```
 Confirmed order ──► 1 Create LR ──► 2 Fill the form ──► 3 Save (Draft)
     ──► 4 Add e-way bill to each LR ──► 5 Finalise ──► 6 Print for the driver
```

## Before you start

| Needed | Why |
|---|---|
| The order is **Confirmed** | You can't make an LR for a pending order (Instant LR is the exception) |
| The order has a **consignee** | The LR needs a receiver |
| **Customer locations** (loading/unloading points) | Chosen on each LR |
| **Goods** and **units** in masters | Chosen on each LR |
| **Own truck:** a trip, or a free truck + driver with valid insurance and licence | To create the trip |
| **Market truck:** the **transporter** in masters | To record who you hired |
| The customer's **invoice number** and **e-way bill** | Needed to finalise |

## Step 1: Start the LR

**From an order (normal):** Operations → Orders → open the **Confirmed** order → **Create LR**.

**Without an order (Instant LR):** Operations → Lorry Receipts → **Create LR**.

## Step 2: Fill the LR form

The form has four sections.

### Section A: Truck & transport

| Field | What to fill |
|---|---|
| **Truck #** \* | Which truck of the order this is (#1, #2, #3…). Trucks already done aren't shown |
| **Transport** | **Road** or **Road & Rail** (see Chapter 9) |
| *(Road & Rail only)* **Source railway branch**, **Source railhead**, **Destination railhead** | The stations for the train part |
| **Payment mode** | **To be Billed** (normal) or **To Pay** |
| **Priority** | Normal, Express or Critical |

### Section B: Parties

| Field | What to fill |
|---|---|
| **Consignor** | Who sends the goods. Filled from the order |
| **Consignee** | Who receives them. Filled from the order |
| **Origin branch** / **Destination branch** | Starting and ending office. Filled from the order |

For an **Instant LR** you choose these yourself.

### Section C: Consignments / LR Lines

Each line becomes **one LR**. Click **Add LR** and fill:

| Field | What to fill |
|---|---|
| **Loading point** | Where the goods are loaded |
| **Unloading point** | Where they are delivered |
| **Goods name**, **Qty** | What is carried and how many (add a row per item) |
| **Total weight** \* and **Unit** \* | e.g. 18 and MT |
| **Invoice no.** \* | The customer's invoice for these goods |
| **Invoice amount (₹)**, **Invoice remark** | Optional |

Add another line if the truck delivers to a second point.

### Section D: Vehicle

Choose **Own Vehicle** or **Market Vehicle**.

- **Own Vehicle:** choose the **Trip**. If there is none, click **Create trip**: fill the truck, driver, route and freight in the pop-up, and it is selected automatically when saved.
- **Market Vehicle:** choose the **transporter**, enter the **vehicle number** and **driver**, then the **Freight amount**, **Advance amount**, **Commission**, **Hamali** and **TDS**. Check the **Total Freight Advance** and **Net Balance Freight** the ERP shows.

## Step 3: Save

Click **Create LR**. You get:
- an **LR group** for this truck, status **Draft**
- one **LR number** for each line, status **Draft**

While it is Draft you can still change it, add LRs or remove LRs.

## Step 4: Add the e-way bill to each LR

Open the LR group. For **each LR**, in the **E-way bill** section, enter:

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **E-way bill number** \* | 12 digits |
| **Generated on** \* | Date it was generated |
| **Expires on** \* | Validity end date |
| **Generated by** | Name or organisation |

Also check that every LR has its **invoice number**.

## Step 5: Finalise

Click **Finalise LR** and enter:

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Base freight (Rs.)** \* | The truck's freight to the customer (usually filled from the order) |
| **Seal number** | The seal put on the truck, e.g. SEAL-0098 |

The group and all its LRs become **Finalised**. They are now **locked**.

If something is missing, the ERP tells you exactly what. For example:

> *LR 00052 is incomplete. Add: invoice number, e-way bill*

Fill the missing items on that LR and click **Finalise LR** again. Before finalising, each LR must have: loading point, unloading point, goods, total weight, weight unit, invoice number and e-way bill.

## Step 6: Print for the driver

Open the LR → **Print / PDF**. Give the copies to the driver. The receiver will sign one copy at delivery.

## Other things you can do

| Task | How | Allowed when |
|---|---|---|
| **Add another LR** to the truck | Group → **Add LR** | Group is **Draft** |
| **Remove an LR** | LR → **Delete** | LR and group are **Draft**. A group keeps at least one LR |
| **Edit** the group | Group → **Edit** | Group is **Draft** |
| **Cancel** the group | Group → **Cancel** (give a reason) | Group is **Draft** only |
| **Hub transfer** (goods change trucks on the way) | Group → **Hold at hub**, then **Dispatch from hub** with the second truck's trip | Group is **Finalised**, nothing delivered yet, and the first trip is **closed** |

**A finalised group can't be cancelled.** If a finalised LR is wrong, don't create a duplicate. Tell your manager or admin.

## Common problems

| Message / problem | Meaning | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "A group can only be created for a Confirmed order" | Order not approved | Get the order approved |
| "Set the order's consignee before creating a group" | Order has no receiver | Edit the order and add the consignee |
| "Vehicle … is already assigned to LR group …" | This market truck/driver is on another open group | Check the other group, or use another truck |
| "Trip … belongs to a different consignor" | The trip is for another customer | Pick or create a trip for this customer |
| "LR … is incomplete. Add: …" | Required details missing | Fill the listed fields, then finalise again |
| "A finalised group cannot be cancelled" | Too late to cancel | Tell your manager or admin |
| Truck # list is empty | All trucks of the order already have LRs | Nothing to do, or check the order's truck quantity |

## Try it yourself (test system only)

1. Take the confirmed 2-truck order from Chapter 7. Click **Create LR** for **Truck #1**.
2. Choose **Road**, **Own Vehicle**, and **Create trip** from the form.
3. Add **two LR lines** with different unloading points. Save. Check you got **2 LR numbers** in one group.
4. Try **Finalise LR** without an e-way bill. Read the error.
5. Add the e-way bills and finalise. Check the status is **Finalised**, then print one LR.
6. For **Truck #2**, choose **Market Vehicle**. Enter freight 60,000 and advance 30,000. Check the **Net Balance Freight**.
