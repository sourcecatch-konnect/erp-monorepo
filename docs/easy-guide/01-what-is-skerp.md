# 1. What is SKERP?

> Last checked: 2026-10-12 · For: anyone new to the ERP

## In one line

**SKERP** is the software SK Translines uses to run its transport work: from the moment a customer asks for a truck, until the customer pays and the money is in the books.

## What SK Translines does

SK Translines moves customers' goods across India. It does this in **two ways**:

| Way | What happens | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **By road** | A truck picks up the goods and drives straight to the customer's delivery point | Truck loads in Pune and unloads in Kolkata |
| **By road + rail** | A truck takes the goods to a **railway station** (railhead). The goods go by **train**. At the other station, a **delivery truck** takes them to the customer | Truck to Amalner station → train to Kolkata → delivery truck to the customer |

It uses **two kinds of trucks**:

| Kind | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Own truck** | The company's own truck, driven by a company driver |
| **Market truck** | A truck hired from an outside **transporter** for one trip. The company pays the transporter |

## Why we need an ERP

Without software, every truck needs papers, phone calls and Excel sheets: who ordered it, which truck went, what freight was agreed, whether the goods arrived, whether the customer paid. Things get lost.

SKERP keeps **all of this in one place**:

- every **order** and every **truck** sent for it
- the **LR** (Lorry Receipt), the transport document for the goods
- where each **truck** is and what it cost (diesel, tolls, driver advance)
- **delivery** of the goods and the signed LR coming back
- the **bill** to the customer and the **payment** received
- payments to **transporters**, **labour** and **drivers**
- the **accounts** (like Tally), filled in automatically

## Who uses it

| Team | What they do in SKERP |
|---|---|
| **Booking / operations** | Take orders, make LRs, send trucks |
| **Rail team** | Plan and load trains (road + rail work) |
| **Branch staff** | Mark goods delivered, receive the signed LR |
| **Billing** | Make bills for customers |
| **Accounts** | Payments, salaries, books of account |
| **Workshop / store** | Truck repairs and spare parts |
| **Managers / owner** | Approve things, look at reports and profit |
| **Admin** | Create users and decide who can do what |

You will only see the menus your job needs. Chapter 4 explains why.

## Words you must know before going further

| Word | Simple meaning |
|---|---|
| **Customer / Consignor** | The company that sends the goods and pays us |
| **Consignee** | The company that receives the goods |
| **Order** | The customer's request: "send me 3 trucks from Pune to Kolkata" |
| **LR (Lorry Receipt)** | The document that travels with the goods. One LR = one consignment |
| **Trip** | One movement of **our own truck** from one place to another |
| **Freight** | The money charged for carrying the goods |
| **Master** | A base record set up once and used everywhere: customer, truck, driver, route, rate |

All other words are in the glossary at the end of this guide.
